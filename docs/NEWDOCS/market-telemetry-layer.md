
# Market Telemetry Layer Specification

## Metrics Matrix Architecture

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The **Market Telemetry Layer** is the foundational analytical layer of the Market Monitoring Platform.

Its responsibility is to observe, measure, and transform raw market data into structured analytical information.

This layer represents the platform's direct connection with market behavior.

It is the only layer responsible for analyzing:

- Price action
- Volume behavior
- Volatility conditions
- Market structure
- Technical indicators
- Market events

All higher-level layers depend on the information generated by this layer.

The Market Telemetry Layer answers:

> **"What is happening in this market, for this asset, on this specific timeframe?"**

---

---

# Information Flow

The Market Telemetry Layer is the first analytical transformation step.

```text
Raw Market Data

        │

        ▼

Market Telemetry Layer

        │

        ▼

Metrics Matrix

        │

        ▼

Signal Correlation Layer
```

The Market Telemetry Layer does not consume information from higher layers.

It produces the foundational market observations consumed by the rest of the system.

---

# Core Responsibility

The Market Telemetry Layer is responsible for creating an accurate and structured observation of market conditions.

It does not answer:

- "Should a trade be opened?"
    
- "Which strategy should be used?"
    
- "What is the market-wide condition?"
    

Those responsibilities belong to higher layers.

Its only responsibility is:

> **Observe the current market state of one asset on one timeframe.**

---

# Primary Component

The Market Telemetry Layer contains one primary analytical component:

```text
Metrics Matrix
```

Every monitored asset and timeframe combination has its own independent Metrics Matrix.

Example:

```text
BTCUSDT

1 Minute

↓

Metrics Matrix
```

```text
BTCUSDT

5 Minutes

↓

Metrics Matrix
```

```text
ETHUSDT

15 Minutes

↓

Metrics Matrix
```

---

# Metrics Matrix Scope

A Metrics Matrix always represents:

```text
One Asset

×

One Timeframe

×

One Market Snapshot
```

Examples:

```text
BTCUSDT × 1 Minute
```

```text
BTCUSDT × 15 Minutes
```

```text
ETHUSDT × 1 Hour
```

The Metrics Matrix never compares different timeframes.

That responsibility belongs to the Signal Correlation Layer.

---

# Metrics Matrix Architecture

The Metrics Matrix is composed of four analytical components.

```text
Metrics Matrix


├── Indicators
│
├── Signals
│
├── Derived Metrics
│
└── Local Confluence
```

Each component represents a different analytical abstraction level.

---

# Component 1 — Indicators

## Purpose

Indicators are mathematical models that transform raw market data into measurable observations.

They represent the lowest analytical level of the platform.

Indicators answer:

> "What does the current market data mathematically show?"

Indicators are calculated from:

- Price
    
- Volume
    
- Volatility
    
- Time
    
- Market structure
    

---

# Indicator Categories

The Metrics Matrix organizes indicators by market dimension.

---

# Component 4 — Local Confluence

## Purpose

Local Confluence is the final output of the Metrics Matrix.

It measures agreement between:

- Indicators
    
- Signals
    
- Derived Metrics
    

inside one timeframe.

---

# Important Distinction

Local Confluence is different from Alignment.

---

## Local Confluence

Question:

> "Do all analytical components inside this timeframe agree?"

Example:

```text
BTCUSDT

5 Minutes


Indicators:

Bullish


Signals:

Bullish


Derived Metrics:

Bullish


↓

Local Confluence:

91%
```

---

## Alignment

Question:

> "Do different timeframes agree?"

This belongs to:

```text
Signal Correlation Layer

└── Alignment Matrix
```

---

# Metrics Matrix Final Output

A complete Metrics Matrix produces:

```text
Metrics Matrix


Indicators

↓

Signals

↓

Derived Metrics

↓

Local Confluence
```

This represents the complete analytical observation of one asset on one timeframe.

---

# Design Principles

---

# Single Timeframe Responsibility

The Metrics Matrix never compares multiple timeframes.

---

# Observation Only

The Metrics Matrix describes the market.

It does not recommend actions.

---

# Deterministic

Identical market inputs must produce identical outputs.

---

# Explainable

Every metric must be traceable to market data.

---

# Modular

Indicators, signals, and derived metrics must be independently extendable.

---

# Strategy Agnostic

The Metrics Matrix describes market behavior without enforcing a trading strategy.

---

# Immutable Snapshots

Every Metrics Matrix represents a specific market snapshot.

Historical snapshots should remain unchanged for analysis and auditing.

---

# No Prediction

The Metrics Matrix does not predict future price movement.

It describes current market conditions.

---

# No Trade Decisions

The Metrics Matrix never determines:

- Entry
    
- Exit
    
- Position size
    
- Stop loss
    
- Take profit
    

Those responsibilities belong to higher-level layers.

---

# Architectural Responsibility

The Market Telemetry Layer answers:

> **"What is happening in this market, for this asset, on this specific timeframe?"**

It transforms raw market data into structured analytical observations.

Every higher layer of the Market Monitoring Platform depends on the accuracy, consistency, and explainability of the Metrics Matrix.
